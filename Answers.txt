### 11
gtRNAdb reports 4 nuclear mitochondrial tRNAs on chromosome 1 and 3 of them are reported in our alignments. In fact, the alignments found even with 100% sequence similarity in region 629432-633533 are missed by tRNAscan-SE (the algorithm that looks for tRNAs). The reason mainly being the differences on the folding. The algorithm relies on predictions on forming the D, T and anticodon loops; mitochondrial tRNAs are more compact and not easily predicted as for nuclear tRNAs. As such, these sequences on nuclear chromosomes are harder to find.
